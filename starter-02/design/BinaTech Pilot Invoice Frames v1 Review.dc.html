C

Visual proposal versus unchanged behaviour

Visual proposal
Register layout: four summary values in a row, eight-column grid at 1024 and above, stacked rows at 768 that keep every label and value. Row selection highlight. Detail drawer grouped into Invoice details, Linked work, Dates and statuses. Create and Edit forms grouped the same way with full labels, 48px fields and the required marker on invoice number only. Status marks over the existing enums: ink square for Cleared and Paid, open ring for Reported and Pending, triangle for Rejected and Overdue, grey for Awaiting IPC and Not Issued; no green, no colour-only meaning. Create and Edit contents proposed within the existing 600px modal; detail contents within the existing 520px drawer. Pending Saving… and inline save error presentation. Disabled Scan and pre-fill with its sandbox note.
Unchanged business behaviour
Refresh, Export, New invoice, New from IPC when a certified IPC exists. Total Invoiced, Paid, Outstanding and ZATCA-cleared computed exactly as today (Σ all; Σ Paid; Σ Pending or Overdue; count Cleared); no new KPI or formula. The invoiceable-now banner only when the existing calculation (certified or paid IPC net total less invoice total, floored at zero) is positive. Detail keeps InvoiceWirLinks and Attachments as they exist; notes appear only as the conditional New-from-IPC pre-fill, not as a saved field. The eight columns. Row click to detail; Edit and Delete with the existing confirmation. The fields invoice_number (required), contractor (read-only from project), element_guid, wir_number, amount SAR including zero, issue_date, due_date, paid_date, zatca_status, payment_status; no added required field or rule; optional values may stay empty. Statuses are recorded data, not enforced certification or payment eligibility. Internal drafts, not legally cleared e-invoices; no Fatoora integration. The Scan and pre-fill action exists in source and stays disconnected in the pilot. The Invoice Edit lifecycle fix is Algene's deliverable and is not drawn here.
